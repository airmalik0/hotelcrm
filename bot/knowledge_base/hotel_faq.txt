# **QUASAR HOTEL — (FAQ)**

---

## **🕒 CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT**

Q: What are the check-in and check-out times?
A: Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability.

Q: Can I check in early or check out late?
A: Subject to availability. Early check-in and late check-out may incur additional charges (typically 50% of room rate). Please contact us in advance.

---

## **📋 REGISTRATION & VISA SUPPORT**

Q: Do you register foreign guests?
A: Yes, we provide mandatory registration for foreign nationals staying in Uzbekistan, as required by law. Please have your passport ready at check-in.

Q: Can you provide visa support documents?
A: Yes, we can provide visa invitation letters for tourists. Please request this at least 2-3 weeks before your arrival with your passport details.

---

## **🚗 PARKING**

Q: Is parking available?
A: Yes, we have limited parking spaces. Please inform us in advance as availability cannot be guaranteed. Parking fees apply.

---

## **📶 WI-FI AND INTERNET**

Q: Is there Wi-Fi?
A: Yes, complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel. Connection details provided at check-in. The connection is suitable for browsing, messaging, and email. Video calls and streaming may be unstable during peak hours.

Q: The Wi-Fi isn't working, what should I do?
A: Please contact reception and we'll assist you. Internet quality depends on the local service provider.

---

## **🍽 DINING**

Q: Is breakfast included?
A: Breakfast inclusion depends on your booking rate. Our continental breakfast includes: tea/coffee, bread, butter, jam, eggs, cheese, vegetables, and seasonal fruits. Served 7:30-10:00.

Q: Can I order food to my room?
A: Limited room service may be available. Please check with reception for menu and hours.

Q: Is there a restaurant?
A: Yes, our restaurant serves Uzbek and European cuisine. Hours may vary seasonally. Reservations recommended for dinner.

---

## **💳 PAYMENT & CURRENCY**

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: Cash (UZS), bank cards (Visa, MasterCard, UZCARD, HUMO). Small processing fee may apply for card payments.

Q: Can I pay in US dollars or euros?
A: UZS is preferred. Foreign currency may be accepted at the daily rate, but change given in UZS only.

Q: Is there currency exchange at the hotel?
A: We don't exchange currency, but can direct you to nearby official exchange offices (within 5-10 minutes walk) with competitive rates.

---

## **🛎 SERVICES AND AMENITIES**

Q: Is there a safe?
A: You can store valuables at reception. Some room categories have in-room safes.

Q: Do you offer laundry service?
A: Yes, laundry service available with 24-48 hour turnaround. Prices vary by item.

Q: Can I get an iron or hairdryer?
A: Hairdryers provided in most rooms. Irons available upon request (subject to availability).

Q: Is there daily housekeeping?
A: Yes, rooms are cleaned daily. Fresh towels and toiletries provided.

---

## **🏨 ROOMS**

Q: Do rooms have air conditioning?
A: Yes, most rooms have air conditioning (essential during summer months May-September when temperatures reach 35-40°C).

Q: Is there hot water?
A: Yes, hot water is available year-round. Note: During annual city maintenance (typically October), hot water may be temporarily unavailable for 1-2 weeks.

Q: What voltage and plug type do you use?
A: 220V, 50Hz. European plug type C/F (round two-pin). Adapters available at reception upon request.

Q: What amenities are in the room?
A: Standard rooms include: bed linens, towels, basic toiletries, TV with local/international channels, air conditioning, tea/coffee set. Mini-bar in some categories (items charged separately).

---

## **🚬 SMOKING POLICY**

Q: Can I smoke in my room?
A: No, all rooms are non-smoking. Designated smoking areas are available on the ground floor/terrace. Smoking in rooms incurs a cleaning fee.

---

## **👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 CHILDREN**

Q: Are children welcome?
A: Yes! Children under 5 stay free when sharing existing beds with parents. Extra bed for older children available for additional charge (subject to availability).

---

## **🚖 TRANSPORTATION**

Q: How do I get to the hotel from the airport?
A: We can arrange transfer (100,000-200,000 UZS depending on vehicle). Or use Yandex Taxi / MyTaxi apps (approximately 50,000-80,000 UZS, 20-40 minutes depending on traffic).

Q: What's the nearest metro station?
A: [Nearest station name] is approximately 10-15 minutes walk. Taxis are convenient and affordable for getting around Tashkent.

Q: How far are main attractions?
A:

* Chorsu Bazaar: ~20 minutes by taxi
* Amir Temur Square: ~15 minutes
* Old City/Khast Imam: ~25 minutes
* Tashkent City Mall: ~20 minutes

---

## **🔐 SECURITY**

Q: Is the hotel secure?
A: Yes, we have 24-hour reception and security personnel. CCTV monitoring in public areas.

---

## **📅 BOOKING & CANCELLATION**

Q: What is your cancellation policy?
A: Cancellation policy varies by rate and season. Standard policy: free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. Later cancellations or no-shows may incur charges. Please check your booking confirmation.

Q: How can I modify my reservation?
A: Contact us by phone or email as soon as possible. Modifications subject to availability.

---

## **🩺 MEDICAL ASSISTANCE**

Q: What if I need medical help?
A: Reception can help arrange medical services. For emergencies dial 103. Nearby clinics and 24-hour pharmacies information available at reception.

---

## **🌆 ADDITIONAL SERVICES**

Q: Can you help arrange city tours?
A: Yes, we can recommend reliable tour operators and help with bookings. Tashkent offers beautiful mosques, museums, bazaars, and Soviet-era architecture.

Q: Is luggage storage available?
A: Yes, complimentary luggage storage at reception before check-in and after check-out.

Q: Can you help order a taxi?
A: Yes, we can call Yandex Taxi or other reliable services. Average wait time 10-20 minutes.
